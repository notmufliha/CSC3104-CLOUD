ScamGuard review

Calls that app users chose to report, with numbers and names already masked. Internal only. Lines you label can be kept to retrain the models; lines you skip or reject are deleted. Anything not reviewed is deleted 30 days after the report.
  1. Type your name (so a teammate can spot-check your labels).
  2. Read the whole call at the top of a report, then decide each line on its own: for each check, Yes if the line does it, No if not. Nothing suspicious = No for all three.
  3. Press Save per line. Skip a line you can't judge. Reject whole report if it's junk or still contains personal details.
Full labelling rules (same as each worker's LABELING.md)
  • Urgency = Yes only when the caller pushes the listener to act now (deadline, threat, "don't hang up"). Ordinary time talk ("I'll call back tomorrow") is No.
  • Money = Yes only when the caller asks for money, card details or a code. "Never share your OTP" is No.
  • Impersonation = Yes only when the caller claims to be from an organisation (police, bank, government). Just mentioning one is No.
  • Judge the line on its own. If you can't tell, press Skip.
  • Privacy check: always Skip (or Reject the report) if anything personal is left: a person's name, an address, or an account number, code or phone number read out as words.
