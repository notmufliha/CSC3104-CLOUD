ScamGuard labelling

Caller lines from calls users chose to report. Already masked; deleted 30 days after the report. Internal only.
Labelling rules (same as each worker's LABELING.md)
  • Urgency = 1 only when the caller pushes the listener to act now (deadline, threat, "don't hang up"). Ordinary time talk ("I'll call back tomorrow") is 0.
  • Money = 1 only when the caller asks for money, card details or a code. "Never share your OTP" is 0.
  • Impersonation = 1 only when the caller claims to be from an organisation (police, bank, government). Mentioning one is 0.
  • Judge the line on its own. If it still contains a person's name or you can't tell, press Skip.
